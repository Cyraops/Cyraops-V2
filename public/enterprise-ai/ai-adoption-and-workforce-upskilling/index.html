What it is

Role-based learning journeys, hands-on labs and mentoring so your people use the AI you already paid for, with adoption and ROI measurement built in. We run cohorts around a live rollout, not a certification business.

Who it's for

L&D, transformation and AI programme leaders

What you get

  • Role-based learning journeys — per role: what changes, what to check, what to stop
  • Hands-on labs and mentoring — your data, your tools, not a slide deck
  • Adoption index and surveys — uptake measured before, during and after rollout
  • AI value and ROI method — hours, cost and outcomes attributed to adoption
  • Policy and safe-use coaching — people learn the guardrails along with the tool
  • Responsible-AI training — bias, privacy and review duties taught, not certified
Selective offering. We take this on in a specific shape — usually one workflow, one entity or one site — and we say so before scoping rather than after. Enterprise AI list it because we can staff it honestly; if the shape does not fit, we will tell you.

How this runs

Scope in a week. A working slice on your data before the full build. You own everything we produce.

Starts with

A 45 minute call and a one page scope, free. No deck, no discovery invoice.
